# Ante unit 3: the payout and the ownership report — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Land unit 3 of the ante spec and close #249: CR 407.2's payout ("at the end of the game, the winner becomes the owner of all the cards in the ante zone") as a game's result is set, and a per-game report of every card whose owner is not the player who began the game owning it (CR 108.3). Subgames pay out before CR 729.5's funnel, and that funnel stops rebuilding a departed player's card that survived in the subgame under another owner (folds #4835). Proved by Amulet of Quoz, Darkpact, Shahrazad, Twincast and a Karn-style restart.

**Architecture:** Every card object carries `Object.identity :: Maybe CardIdentity` (`Pawl.Types.CardIdentity`: a serial taken from the id the card was first minted under, and its CR 108.3 starting owner), set where a card is minted, carried by `newIncarnation`, a restart and a subgame, and `Nothing` for a token, emblem, ability or copy. `Pawl.Engine.Interchangeable` compares an identity by its starting owner only. A result is set through one door, `Departure.settle`, which calls `Ante.payOut` (CR 407.2, through unit 2's `Game.setOwner`); its three writers are `Departure.leaveGameTogether`, `Sba`'s pass and `Engine.checkMandatoryLoop`. `Ante.ownershipChanges` is the report, and a scenario `View` (`OwnershipChanges`) renders it. `Setup.funnelBack` tells a departed owner's surviving subgame card from the parent card it came from by identity.

**Tech Stack:** Haskell (GHC via the repo's nix flake), cabal, tasty.
**Spec:** docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-08-ante-design.md (its unit-3 section was corrected in the commit that adds this plan; the corrections are listed under "Divergences" below).

## Review Focus

The five input classes most likely to bite. Each has a test in its owning task.

1. **A departed player's ante card in a subgame someone else wins.** Three seats, two leave inside the subgame (CR 800.4a reaches them, CR 800.4n keeps their ante cards), the third wins and is paid. Before this unit `funnelBack` hands each departed player their whole main-game library back AND the winner the paid card: the card exists twice. Test: Task 6, GameSpec "CR 407.2/729.5 gameplay: a Shahrazad subgame's ante goes to its winner's main-game library".
2. **A partial draw, then a winner.** A player who draws leaves (CR 104.4a / 801.16 are departures for the rest), their ante card stays (CR 800.4n), and the eventual winner owns it. Test: Task 5, AnteSpec "CR 800.4n/407.2 a player who draws leaves their ante card to the eventual winner".
3. **A team win.** `TeamWon` pays nothing (elided, issue filed in Task 5). Tests: Task 5, AnteSpec "CR 104.2c a team's win pays out no ante card"; Task 6, the teamed Shahrazad case.
4. **Two cards that differ only in their identity.** Their serials always differ, so an unmasked whole-Object compare would stop `Interchangeable` from ever eliding a choice between two identical cards; and two cards with different STARTING owners must stay distinguishable, since the report tells them apart. Test: Task 1, AnteSpec "CR 108.3 two cards differing only in their identity's serial are interchangeable, and not when their starting owners differ".
5. **A copy built by a record update of a card.** `Resolve/Effect`'s stack-copy and castable-copy builds start from the copied card's object, so without an explicit `Nothing` the copy inherits its identity and, owned by the copier, is reported as an ownership change. Test: Task 4, CopySpec "CR 707.10 a copy of a spell is no card, so the ownership report never lists it".

The spec's own proofs (the winner owns every ante card and the report lists them; a draw reports none; a Darkpact-changed card is reported; a restart pays nothing) are Tasks 3, 5 and 6.

## The design question: a card identity, or only a starting owner

The spec planned `Object.startingOwner :: PlayerId`. The recommended shape is `Object.identity :: Maybe CardIdentity`, a serial plus that starting owner, in ONE field.

- **Cost.** The same construction sites either way (eleven engine `Object.MkObject`s, `Codec.Object`, `Reversal.objectWith`, and about thirty test sites, five of them positional), plus: a type module and its codec; one line in `Interchangeable.objects` (Review Focus 4); `representComponent`'s and the meld's identity (Task 4); and about fifteen lines of `funnelBack` (Task 6). About two tasks more than the starting-owner field.
- **Closes:** #249 and #4835. Unit 3 itself needs #4835's fix: its own payout makes the duplicate in Review Focus 1 reachable in every ante subgame of three or more seats where a player leaves, and `castShahrazad`, the helper the subgame tests use, has three seats.
- **Does not close:** #4829 (a departed player's ante card that came into the subgame from OUTSIDE it). That needs `OutsideObject` to carry the identity across a main-game crossing and the sideboard pool to be debited by the card that came back; both are left, with a comment on #4829. #4837 (from anywhere through a merged permanent's departure) needs `MergeComponent` to carry each component's identity; Task 4 files that as its own issue and links it as #4837's blocker. `ObjectRef.FromAnywhere` keeps the per-turn `Moved` log: a serial search would need the departed object's identity in `LastKnown`, and gains nothing a card in the pool reaches.
- **Risks.** (a) `Interchangeable`: a missed mask turns every identical-card choice into a prompt; loud (ScenarioSpec's two-Forests case and many scenarios redden), and pinned by Review Focus 4. (b) A copy built by record update inheriting an identity: three such builds exist, Task 1 writes `Nothing` at each, and Review Focus 5 pins the one a card reaches. (c) Merged and melded permanents: one object, several cards. Their components come apart sharing the merged object's identity, which is the starting-owner field's hole too; filed in Task 4. (d) The serial is the minted `ObjectId`'s number. That is unique because the id supply only counts up and a subgame continues the parent's supply (`funnelBack` takes the max), and it adds no supply to `GameState`.

### Alternative: `Object.startingOwner :: PlayerId`

What changes under it: Task 1 adds a `PlayerId` field and no type module, codec or `Interchangeable` mask (whole-Object Eq is already right: two cards with different starting owners are distinguishable). Task 4 still needs the copies and components to be read (a component copies the merged object's starting owner). Task 6 drops the `funnelBack` change, so its won-subgame test runs at TWO seats (alice 9, bob 6: no CR 800.4a departure) and the three-seat case stays wrong: #4835 stays open, gets a comment that the payout now reaches it without Darkpact, and is cited at `Ante.payOut` as well as `funnelBack`. Review Focus 1 and 4 then pin nothing; replace them with the two-seat subgame and a restart after an ownership change.

## Divergences from the spec (corrected in the spec in this plan's commit; the PR body repeats them)

1. **`Object.identity :: Maybe CardIdentity`, not `Object.startingOwner`.** See the design question above.
2. **The report is `Pawl.Engine.Ante.ownershipChanges`, not a new `Pawl.Engine.Ownership` module.** One function; CR 407.3 makes ante the only thing that changes an owner, and `Pawl.Engine.Ante` already holds CR 407's engine reads. Its shape is the spec's: `Map ObjectId (PlayerId, PlayerId)`, starting owner then owner.
3. **One door sets the result: `Departure.settle`.** The spec named the payout "when `GameState.result` becomes `Won`". There are three writers (`Departure.leaveGameTogether`, `Sba.checkStateBasedActions`'s pass, `Engine.checkMandatoryLoop`'s draw); each already keeps an earlier result (CR 104.1). Paying in `Engine.playGame` instead would miss every scenario, since `Pawl.Scenario.playOut` drives `Engine.runStep` and stops on a result.
4. **The payout reads the ante zone, not `GameSettings.ante`.** A card can only be in the ante in a game played for ante (CR 407.3, unit 1's lint and bar), so the gate would be dead.
5. **A scenario `View` renders the report** (`OwnershipChanges`, `[card, starting owner, owner]` triples). The spec's Darkpact proof is a scenario, and no `Check` reads an owner's history.
6. **#4835 is folded, #4829 and #4837 are not.** See the design question.
7. **The report sees only the cards still in the game.** A card that left the game with a departed player (CR 800.4a) is gone from `GameState.objects`; filed in Task 3.
